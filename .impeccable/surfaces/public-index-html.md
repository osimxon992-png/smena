# Landing: Smena

Scope: public/index.html (Persuade) and the app shell public/app.html (Operate, inherits the world with restraint).
Audience: Tashkent students looking for one-day paid work; wedding halls, restaurants, warehouses and pick-up points needing people for a day.
Action: workers open the shift list and apply; businesses register to post a shift.
Proof on hand: live open shifts from /api/shifts when any exist; otherwise example shifts labelled "Namuna". No stats, partners or reviews exist; never invent them. Pricing is 15% of the worker's pay, charged to the business per filled shift.
Constraints: Uzbek Latin copy, no purple, mobile first, PWA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Shifts are sold the way Chorsu sells goods: the pay is written large on a cardboard price tag hung on twine across the stall, nothing hidden. Refuses the split hero with a product shot and the row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched kraft cardboard ground with fibre grain; marker-black ink; red marker reserved for pay on paper tags; Chorsu-dome teal for the rules and trust signs; off-white price-tag paper with a punched hole and twine; translucent tape strips; spent or full shifts fade to grey card. Display voice is a felt-marker face, body a sturdy grotesk with tabular figures on one numeric scale.

STORY: The visitor sees real shifts and their pay first, learns the one rule (accepted and absent means blocked), follows one tag from open to done, sees what a business pays, then applies or posts a shift.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cardboard board. Marker headline at top left, about 8vw. A twine line crosses the full width; five price tags hang from it, each with role, red pay, place, time, slots left and an apply action. Bottom left, a taped paper note with one offer sentence, primary "Barcha smenalar" and secondary "Menga ishchi kerak". Bottom right, a teal sign: "Kelmagan bloklanadi."

Signature interaction: the tags swing on the twine with scroll velocity; one tag is stamped through its states as the story section scrolls. Motion grammar: marker-wipe reveal on section headings, rubber-stamp hits on state changes, nothing else animates on its own.

FORM: Bozor taxtasi (Chorsu hand-lettered cardboard price signs), position 4 of 7, seed key 3b03c8ff.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
